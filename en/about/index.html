About

13 years of guiding teams to decisions.

I'm based in Prague and Amsterdam. Teams bring me in when product decisions are overdue, or when they keep going in circles.

How I work

  • I lead the discussion and sketch at the same time.
  • In a single session, I keep track of both the big picture and the details.
  • Between sessions, I write. I'd rather send a draft than a meeting invite.
  • I direct the visual design; a designer draws the pixels.
  • I write down every major decision and why we made it, so we don't keep revisiting it.

Who I work best with

  • People who care more about the result than about being right.
  • Teams where mistakes are costly: regulators, licenses, health, people's savings.
  • Founders who stay involved.

What I care about

  • We solve the real problem, then move on.
  • Results matter, and so do the people behind them.

So you know up front

What I'm not

  • I'm not a metrics-focused PM. I get decisions made.
  • I'm not an agency.
  • I'm not an extra pair of hands in Figma.
