Coinmate · 2023–2026 · Regulated crypto exchange

A design system first, then a new brand identity.

How it happened

Timeline

  1. Where it was stuck

    No design system

    The company had no design system and no product design team. I built the system first. When the rebrand request came, the exploration took days instead of weeks, and we could test several concepts instead of one.

  2. Step 1

    A scalable design system

    It built on the existing style, so nothing started from scratch.

  3. Step 2

    One system for iOS and Android

    The mobile apps got the same system, so users recognize the brand everywhere.

  4. Step 3

    Exchanges and banks

    I analyzed crypto exchanges and banks that are starting to offer crypto. Then I designed the most complex screens for mobile and desktop first, to make sure the system would scale.

  5. Step 4

    Prototypes for the whole company

    Claude Design came out in April 2026. In May, I connected the design system in Figma to Claude and set up automatic deploys to Vercel. From the end of May, product managers built prototypes from the design system themselves and tested ideas with engineering and the board. Anyone in the company can open the link, even without a Claude account.

  6. Step 5

    Merch made in-house

    I prepared the logo and the mascot in Figma Weave so merch could go straight to production. That removed the loop of brief, supplier rounds, approval, and budget. People inside the company learned the process and now make the materials themselves.

  7. Result

    A new brand on the same system

    The design system stayed, and the new brand identity is built on it: a new logo and colors, the same name. I was responsible for the visual design.
