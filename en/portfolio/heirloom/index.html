Heirloom · 2026 · Digital legacy, a pivot from B2C to B2B

A new business direction in 4 iterations.

How it happened

Product definition pipeline

Timeline

  1. Where it was stuck

    Everyone expected Figma screens

    The company was pivoting from B2C to B2B and needed to pin down the product scope and spec. Everyone expected screens in Figma. Instead, I set up a process with a working prototype in every iteration.

  2. Step 1

    Scope with leadership

    I facilitated the questions that defined the project scope.

  3. Step 2

    AI prototype from code

    The prototype was built with AI from the existing repository and design system.

  4. Step 3

    Fast iterations with comments

    Comments went directly into the prototype. It covered every state, including edge cases.

  5. Result

    New direction in 4 sessions

    Disagreements that used to surface during development were settled during prototyping. We settled the new direction in 4 sessions.
